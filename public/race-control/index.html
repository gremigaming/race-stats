GreMi Race Control

Uses YouTube API Services

GreMi Race Control is the private stream tool of the YouTube channel GreMi_Gaming (sim racing). Only the channel owner signs in, and it only works on that one channel.

What it does

  • Shorts uploads: uploads short clips that GreMi_Gaming edits from his own streams to his own channel, on a schedule, with a title, description and thumbnail he approved first.
  • Live chat: while GreMi_Gaming is live, it reads his own live chat to answer his chat commands and posts his own timed messages (stream reminders, sponsor codes).

It never accesses other channels, never downloads other people's videos and never stores YouTube data apart from its own encrypted sign-in token.
