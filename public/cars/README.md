Top-down F1 car sprites (v2, from the Texas Turn 1 incident mock-up). One SVG per team, nose pointing +x, origin at the car centre, viewBox in metres (length 5.6 m, width 2.0 m; also in data-length-m / data-width-m and index.json). Team colour parts: `body` is the main bodywork (with darker flanks and a lighter spine), `accent` the front wing element, the sidepod flashes and a rear wing element, `accent2` the engine-cover stripe and the airbox details, `number` the race number colour (not drawn: no number in these files); tyres and carbon parts are fixed. For a team without its own file, use `generic.svg`.
